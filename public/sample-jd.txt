Senior Frontend Engineer — Nebula Systems

ABOUT THE ROLE
Nebula Systems is looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to lead frontend architecture for our core product dashboard. You'll work closely with our design team, backend engineers, and customers to build and maintain a best-in-class data analytics platform.

RESPONSIBILITIES
• Lead frontend architecture for core product dashboard
• Collaborate with design team to build and maintain design system
• Write and maintain comprehensive unit and integration tests
• Mentor junior engineers and lead bi-weekly frontend guild meetings
• Integrate with backend GraphQL APIs and define shared frontend/backend contracts
• Drive CI/CD pipeline improvements to reduce deployment lead time

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
• 4+ years of experience with React
• Strong TypeScript proficiency
• Experience with GraphQL clients (Apollo or urql)
• Track record of improving test coverage and code quality
• Experience mentoring or leading small engineering teams

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
• Experience with Next.js
• AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda)
• Design Systems / Storybook
• WebSockets or real-time data experience
• Docker and containerization

TECHNICAL STACK
React, TypeScript, GraphQL, Apollo Client, Jest, GitHub Actions, AWS, Docker, Figma

ABOUT US
Nebula Systems is a B2B SaaS company building analytics infrastructure for enterprise data teams. Our platform processes 10M+ events/day and is used by 500+ enterprise customers globally.
